What is CALELLA?

Calella is a nice little city in Spain forty-five minutes away from Barcelona airport. It is part of the "Costa del Brava."

What makes Calella unique?

It is perfect for family trips with young children because it is quiet and not too crowded. It has a beautiful beach and a lot of accommodations very close to the shore, making it easy to enjoy the view. It also has charming architecture worth discovering. Calella also benefits from great transport links to Barcelona, Girona, and other cities.

Places to visit in Calella

  • Parc Dalmau
  • Church of Santa Maria
  • Platja de Calella
